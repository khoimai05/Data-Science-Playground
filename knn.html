How KNN works

KNN has no training phase. To predict at a new point, we find the K closest labeled points and take a majority vote of their classes.

Small K (e.g. 1): flexible, captures fine structure, but sensitive to noise. Large K: smoother boundaries, may underfit.

Try different K values and distance metrics (Euclidean vs Manhattan) to see how the decision boundary changes.
