How gradient descent works

We fit a line y = mx + b by minimizing the mean squared error (MSE): the average of (prediction - actual)² over all points.

Instead of solving directly, we start with a guess (e.g. horizontal line) and take small steps downhill. Each step: compute how wrong we are, then update m and b in the direction that reduces the error.

The gradient tells us which way is "down." We move m and b opposite to the gradient, scaled by a learning rate. Repeating this, the line settles into the best fit.
