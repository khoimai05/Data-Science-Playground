principal component analysis

find the axes of maximum variance

How it works

PCA finds orthogonal directions that capture the most variance in the data. The first principal component (PC1) is the line along which the data varies most.

It does this by computing the covariance matrix of centered data, then finding its eigenvectors. The eigenvector with the largest eigenvalue points in the direction of maximum spread.

Projections onto PC1 give the best 1D summary of 2D data — minimizing reconstruction error.
